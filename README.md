# Manifesto Machines

Eighty-six music-making tools (and counting), each built from two things:

1. **A real source idea**: an artist's manifesto, a theory of composition, a working method (found-sound production, generative systems), or an idea from nature or philosophy, whose ideas become the rules of the music system. Futurism only accelerates, Dada cuts your loop up in a hat, Dogme 95 forbids effects, Auto-Destructive Art destroys the sample as it plays.
2. **A graphic design style**, which drives the whole look: type, grid, colour and layout. Plakatstil, Swiss grid, condensed magazine type, cut-paper film titles, Memphis, brutalist web and more. Each style is named by its technique, with a short "inspired by" credit.

The idea is to learn about art, manifestos and graphic design while making music. Every tool has a **LEARN** panel covering the manifesto (author, year, key ideas, public-domain quotes or our own paraphrase, and how the machine reads it) and the design style (what inspired it, when, hallmarks, and works to look up). See [CREDITS.md](CREDITS.md), [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md) and [RIGHTS-LOG.md](RIGHTS-LOG.md).

**Play them:** https://anorthernman-developer.github.io/manifesto-machines/ (a searchable catalogue: filter by category, source, design style, deep instruments or Gemini; press `/` to search)

**Stand-alone live apps:** [Emotional Frameworks Live](https://emotional-frameworks-live.netlify.app) ([repo](https://github.com/anorthernman-developer/emotional-frameworks-live)) is machine 77 turned into a performance app. Play the instrument on your laptop while an OUTPUT window on the projector shows audio-reactive VJ / digital-signage visuals you control live: scenes, themes, text, brand, blackout and freeze. [Fifty-Three Cells](https://fifty-three-cells.netlify.app) ([repo](https://github.com/anorthernman-developer/fifty-three-cells)) is machine 79 turned into a performance app: up to 12 players on 53 app-written cells, with an OUTPUT window of ensemble-driven visuals (player lanes, cell strip, op-art pulse, phase wheel, graphic score, big type, signage, sample scope).

## Using them

- Each tool is **one standalone HTML file** (`tools/NN-slug.html`). Download it and open it from your disk in Chrome or Edge; it works offline (Google Fonts load when online, with system fallbacks).
- Press **PLAY** (or Space). Every tool plays built-in synthesised sounds straight away.
- Drop your own samples anywhere on the page, or use **SAMPLES** / **FOLDER** (for example `F:\01 Splice Sounds`; a random dozen is used from a folder). WAV, AIFF/AIFC (uncompressed), MP3, OGG and FLAC all work. AIFF is read by a small built-in decoder when the browser can't.
- **● REC** records the output to a WAV file. **LEARN** (or L) opens the notes.
- A limiter on the master output keeps everything at a safe level.
- Fifteen tools (10, 13, 36, 41, 48, 52, 56, 61, 62, 67, 71, 76, 77, 81, 83) can optionally ask Google Gemini for new material. Paste a Google AI Studio key into the box. **Your key stays in your browser:** it is stored only in your browser's `localStorage` (shared with SR-12 and the other tools as `sr12.geminiKey`), sent only to Google, and never to us; **FORGET KEY** deletes it. **No key is ever stored in this repo**: a pre-commit hook (`git config core.hooksPath .githooks`), a gitleaks GitHub Action and GitHub secret scanning with push protection all check for keys. Without a key they use their built-in version.

### The deep instruments (52 onwards)

From 27 October there are five new tools a day: one found-sound production method, one generative system, one theory of composition, one from nature or philosophy, and one art manifesto. They are built on a second engine, the **deck** (`src/deck.js`), so they can be customised for your own sounds:

- **SOUNDS** (D opens the deck): 4 to 9 named sample slots. Each slot loads a WAV, AIFF, MP3 or a whole folder (with "next file" to step through it) and has pitch, gain, start, end, reverse, filter, pan, and reverb and delay sends. A waveform shows the region.
- **PRESETS**: save your settings and your sounds' slot settings to the browser (localStorage), or export and import them as JSON. Each tool has a few factory presets.
- **MIDI**: Web MIDI input. Notes do something musical in each tool, and MIDI learn maps any knob or fader to any slider.
- **KEYS** (or `?`): the keyboard shortcuts sheet.
- LEARN has a "Try this" list of three ideas for using your own sounds.

They still play within a few seconds of pressing PLAY.

### Standing rules (from 1 November 2026)

1. **No imitation of named people.** Gemini prompts, titles and UI never ask for work "in the style of", "like" or "after" a named living or recently deceased person. Prompts describe the technique instead.
2. **Technique first.** Tools, themes, presets and kickers are named by the technique or style (for example "Grunge layout", "Generative systems"), not by a person.
3. **Short, neutral credits only.** A person, studio or movement may appear once as a short factual "Inspired by …" credit. No biography, personal details or anecdotes, and nothing that suggests endorsement.
4. **No copyrighted quotes.** Only quote texts that are in the public domain in the UK (author died in 1955 or earlier), marked "public domain; our translation". Everything else is our own paraphrase, marked "In our words".
5. **Keys stay in the browser.** A Gemini key is pasted by the visitor, kept only in `localStorage`, sent only to Google, and never committed. Run the secret scan before every commit.
6. **Keep the START/END trim controls** on every sample slot.

## How it's built

`src/kit.js` and `src/kit.css` are a small shared engine (audio graph and limiter, fallback sounds, sample loading with the AIFF reader, WAV recorder, LEARN panel, optional Gemini box). `src/tools/NN-slug.js` holds each tool's metadata, CSS, HTML and music system. `python3 build.py` inlines everything into one self-contained file per tool and regenerates `index.html`, `tools.json`, `LEDGER.md` and the list above. `test/run.js` is a headless Chrome test (see `DEVLOG.md`).

`src/deck.js` and `src/deck.css` are the deck for the deep instruments (#52 on). `test/deep.js` tests the deck: slots, AIFF and WAV, reverse, presets, JSON export and import, MIDI, and a 10-second soak. New tools are added five a day from #52 (one a day for #51). [LEDGER.md](LEDGER.md) lists every manifesto and design homage already used, so none repeat.
