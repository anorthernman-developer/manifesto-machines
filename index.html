Manifesto Machines

51 music-making tools. Each one turns a real artist's manifesto into the rules of a music system, and is designed as a homage to a graphic designer or design style. Open one, press PLAY (it plays built-in sounds straight away), drop in your own samples (WAV, AIFF, MP3), record to WAV, and press LEARN to read about the manifesto and the design.

Every tool is one standalone HTML file that works offline from your disk in Chrome or Edge. Source on GitHub · Ledger
