Manifesto Machines · 101

A growing catalogue of music-making instruments for the browser. Each one turns a source idea into the rules of a music system: an art manifesto, a theory of composition, an idea from nature or philosophy, or a production technique such as found-sound production or generative systems. Each also has its own graphic design style, with a short credit to what inspired it. Press PLAY and it makes sound straight away; load your own samples, record to WAV and press LEARN to read about the idea and the design.

From no. 52 the tools are deeper instruments, with a DECK (press D) of sample slots (pitch, gain, start and end, reverse, filter, pan, reverb and delay), presets you can save, load, export and import, MIDI in with MIDI learn, and a keyboard sheet (press ?). Every tool is one standalone HTML file that works offline in Chrome or Edge. Source on GitHub · Ledger · catalogue.json
